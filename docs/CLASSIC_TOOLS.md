# Classic magical tools

## Thaumometer and Goggles of Revealing

Craft a Thaumometer at a regular crafting table to start discovery. Use it on a block or creature, aim at a dropped item, or crouch with a specimen in the offhand and use the instrument. Use it without a target for local Vis and Flux. Crouch with an empty offhand for readings from the local and loaded cardinal chunks. Aimed readings cover nodes and supported alchemical equipment. The legacy Field Lens, Attuned Lens, and Aura Surveyor are absent from new crafting and the creative tab. Existing stacks keep their IDs in storage and automatically exchange in player inventory, preserving item components and saved discoveries.

Craft Goggles of Revealing at the Arcane Workbench after Arcane Foundations. Use two Thaumometers in the outer slots of the top row and three leather below. The recipe costs 24 Vis. Wear them in the head slot to read a Crucible or a supported aura-relevant source without holding a tool and see brighter nodes directly in the world. The client displays a server reading. [Thaumcraft beta journal text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Azanor/thaumcraft-beta/master/en_us.lang)

## Wand

Craft the starter wand with a stick between two iron nuggets in a vertical line. The item has six independent, initially empty Vis reservoirs. Its tooltip displays the six reserves with a capacity of 25 per aspect. Discoveries reveal their names and colors. Minecraft's item data component persists the amounts when an inventory is saved and synchronizes the item to clients. The cap is a Thaumic Legacy gameplay value.

Wands start empty in survival. Study an aura node with a Thaumometer, then hold use while aiming a Wand at a node. The node supplies one primal reserve at a time. Chunk Vis and Flux remain independent.
